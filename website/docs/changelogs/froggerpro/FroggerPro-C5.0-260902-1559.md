# FroggerPro-C5.0-260902-1559

## Update changelogs
Welcome to Nothing OS 5.0 (Beta)  
Thank you for joining the Nothing OS 5.0 Open Beta. This release introduces some new features and a refreshed visual design, blending Nothing OS elements with Android 17.  
This version is still in development, with more features and updates to come. We value your feedback and suggestions as we continue to refine the experience.  
Please back up your data, including photos, call logs, and contacts, before upgrading. Here's what's new:  

### New Features
🔮 Introduced the Glyph app, designed specifically for the Glyph Interface, offering quicker access and a refreshed interactive experience. Find it on the home screen or app drawer.  
🗄️ Quick Settings and the notification panel now support split, giving you a cleaner layout and easier access. Enable it in Settings > Notifications > General > Notification and quick settings.  
✅ Arranging home screen icons in bulk is now available. Try it out via long-pressing the home screen and tapping on "Arrange."  
🚥 Introduces a new three-colour status bar indicator for scenes of sensitive access, location, AI processing, letting you know device activity instantly.  

### Visual Enhancements
🎨 Lock screen customisation now supports setting up a colour for your clock face.  
🕰️ Added a new lock screen clock face.  
🎴 Updated wallpaper gallery with a new style wallpaper.  
🛜 Refined the status bar icons with a clearer and more consistent style and subtle animations.  
🖋️ Refined the system design with the newly introduced Geist typeface and refreshed Settings UI for clearer readability and a more cohesive look.  
🌌 Improved interactions and transition animations across various scenarios for a more fluid experience.  

### Fresh Widgets
🪆 Introduced Collector widgets, a playful new way to turn your photos into different-style decorations on your home screen.  
⏱️ Away Time widgets are now available, effortlessly tracking your time away from the screen to help you gain better insights into your daily habits.  
⏳ Glyph Timer is now supported in the widget drawer, offering you handy interaction on your home screen.  

### Camera Enhancements
🎥 Added Dual-view Video to record with the front and rear cameras simultaneously.  
✨ Improved recording, zoom, close-up shooting, QR scanning, Google Lens, and Motion Photo playback.  
📷 Refreshed the Camera UI, controls, feedback, and animations.  

### General Improvements and Bug Fixes
🌑 Expanded Dark theme is now available, automatically applying the Dark theme to most apps that don't support it natively. Adjust it anytime in Settings > Display > Dark theme > Expanded.  
🔆 Enhanced HDR brightness is now available, allowing you to customize HDR intensity for a comfortable viewing experience. Set it up in Settings > Display > Enhanced HDR brightness.  
🎧 Essential Voice can now be triggered via the Ear (3) Super Mic while using the keyboard.  
👆 Depth (Beta) wallpapers now support pinching to shrink, giving more flexibility for your operation.  
💊 Refined Live Updates with an enhanced status bar experience for ongoing activities.  
👻 Introduced Noto 3D Emoji in Gboard for a more vivid look and expression.  
🏠 Improved interaction with the lock screen shortcuts for a more natural experience.  
🎧 The Bluetooth in Quick Settings now supports instant connection or disconnection with a single tap of the device icon.  

## Notes
The device may temporarily heat up and increase power consumption during the update and will quickly return to normal after the process is complete.
